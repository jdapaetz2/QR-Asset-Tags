Nameplate band in the analytics dialect: PlateLabel "FLEET ANALYTICS", the mono stamp is the active date range, and the headline is a derived insight sentence (period totals + top asset), never a page label. RangeControl (7/30/90) drives every module below. Chart grammar: iron history bars, one brass current-period bar per chart, zero days as 2px stubs, mono value chip on hover, mono ticks, no gridlines. Lifetime-total stat cards are retired; totals live in chart headers.
