## TRAILER-014: Big Tex 35SA-12

**Headline** (already cleared): 12 ft single-axle utility trailer with a 4 ft ramp gate. It tows on a 2-inch ball rated for 2,995 lb or more and has no brakes.

### Needs your OK

**Quick start**
- 1. 2 in ball, 2,995 lb or more. Not cracked, worn or loose.
- 2. Seat, latch, pin. Lift-test ≤ 1 in, then jack fully up.
- 3. Cross the chains under the coupler, hooks up from below.
- 4. Plug in the 4-way. Check tail, brake and turn lights.
- 5. Tires undamaged, cold pressure per VIN label, lug nuts tight.
- 6. Load hitched, level: about 60% in front, heavy over axle.
- 7. Strap to the tie-down loops. Gate latched, pin if fitted.
- 8. Every 50 mi or hour: check coupler, chains, load, gate.

**Safety**
- Don't move until the coupler is pinned, the chains are on and the jack is up.
- No riders.
- No flammable, explosive or poisonous loads.
- 60 mph maximum, in ideal conditions only.
- If it sways: ease off the gas, steer as little as possible, don't brake.
- Trailer and load together: 2,995 lb at most.

**Troubleshooting**
- Lights not working: check the 4-way plug is fully connected.
- Coupler won't latch: check the ball is 2 inches. Don't tow until it locks.
- Anything cracked, loose or damaged: don't tow. Use Request Support.

**Return**
- Return the straps, chain pins and spare tire.
- Unhitch: block the tires, unplug, unhook the chains, open the coupler, jack down on firm ground.
- Use Return checklist when you drop it off.

**Left blank:** Fuel and power. This trailer has neither.

## TRAILER-2041: Big Tex 14LP-14

**Headline** (already cleared): 14 ft tandem-axle dump trailer with electric brakes, breakaway and a battery-powered hoist. It tows on a 2-5/16 inch ball rated for 14,000 lb or more, with a brake controller in the truck.

### Needs your OK

**Quick start**
- 1. 2-5/16 in ball, 14,000 lb or more. Seat, latch, pin, test.
- 2. Cross the chains. Breakaway cable to the truck; pull-test it.
- 3. Plug in the 7-way. Check lights; test brakes under 5 mph.
- 4. Before dumping: level, firm ground, people clear, no wires overhead.
- 5. Chain the doors open; open the control box.
- 6. Raise to about half, look behind; then three-quarters, look again.
- 7. Load stuck: lower the body, free it by hand.
- 8. Body down, doors latched, controls locked. Then drive.
- Tires: undamaged, cold pressure per VIN label, lug nuts tight.
- Load hitched and level; spread loose material evenly.

**Safety**
- Never go under a raised body.
- Never push the hoist with a jack or machine; unload by hand.
- Don't fully raise a loaded body.
- A raised body near power lines can electrocute. Check overhead before raising.
- Don't move until the coupler is pinned, the chains and breakaway cable are on and the jack is up.
- No riders, and no flammable, explosive or poisonous loads.
- 60 mph maximum, in ideal conditions only.
- Stay at the controls while the body is up.
- Stay within the payload on the trailer's decal.

**Fuel and power**
- Hoist: 12 V battery with an onboard 110 V charger and a 5 W solar charger.
- The breakaway battery drains fast with its pin out: put the pin straight back.

**Troubleshooting**
- Hoist won't lift: unload some by hand, then use Request Support.
- Load stuck: lower the body and free it by hand.
- Lights or brakes dead: check the 7-way plug.
- Breakaway test fails: don't tow. Use Request Support.

**Return**
- Return the straps, chain pins, spare tire and hoist remote.
- Body down, doors latched, controls locked.
- Unhitch: block the tires, unplug, unclip the breakaway, unhook the chains, open the coupler, lower the jack on firm ground.
- Use Return checklist when you drop it off.

## EXCAVATOR-017: Bobcat E20

### Needs your OK

**Headline** (changed: the draft said "Read the Start-Up Guide before you start.")

Bobcat E20 compact excavator with canopy. Northridge walks you through start-up and the controls at handover.

**Quick start** (new: the drafted steps need Bobcat's manual, so this follows the Cat page. If you have the manual, I write the steps from it instead.)

Before operating, go through start-up and the controls with Northridge staff at handover. Bobcat's Operation and Maintenance Manual is linked under Manual.

**Safety**
- Keep people out of the swing area; never lift over anyone.
- Slopes: 15° across or up, 25° down or backing up. No swinging on a slope.
- No riders.
- Never work the controls from the ground.
- Keep hands and feet inside while the machine is on.
- No smoking while fueling.
- Stay within the lift chart on the machine.

**Troubleshooting**
- Something wrong (a warning light, leak or odd noise): stop, lower the bucket to the ground, shut down and use Request Support.

**Return**
- Return the keys and any attachments.
- Leave it with the bucket on the ground, engine off, key out and console up.
- Use Return checklist when you drop it off.

**Left blank:** Fuel and power. Fuel type and tank size come from Bobcat's manual.
