## Quick guide (renter)

1. Check that the tow vehicle's hitch ball is 2 inches and rated for at least 2,995 lb, and that the ball, hitch and coupler have no cracks, wear or looseness. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual, p. 6, 1.2.4 warning; pp. 36-37, "Coupling to the Tow Vehicle"; 35SA Single Axle Utility Trailer (product page), Features & Options, Coupler
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**: Sourced; knowledgeable review outstanding.
2. Grease the ball, open the coupler, raise it above the ball with the jack and back the tow vehicle under it.
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual, p. 37, "Prepare the coupler and hitch"
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
3. Lower the coupler fully onto the ball, close the latch and put the pin or lock through it. Test it: use the trailer jack to raise the rear of the tow vehicle 1 inch (no more), then lower and wind the jack fully up. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual, p. 38, "Couple the trailer to the tow vehicle"
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
4. Cross the safety chains under the coupler and hook them to the tow vehicle's frame (not to the hitch unless it has loops for chains), with the S-hooks put in from underneath. Leave enough slack to turn, but not enough to drag. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual, p. 7, 1.2.5; p. 39, "Rig the safety chains"
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
5. Connect the 4-way plug and check the tail, brake and turn lights with a helper. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual, p. 12, 1.2.13; pp. 40-41, "Connect the Electrical Cables"
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
6. Look for tire damage, set the pressure printed on the trailer's VIN label (front left) while the tires are cold, and check that the lug nuts are tight. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual, pp. 8-9, 1.2.8
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**: Torque value unavailable (see spec lug-torque).
7. Load with the trailer hitched and on level ground: about 60% of the load in the front half, heavy items on the floor over the axle, and no more than 2,995 lb for trailer and load together. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual, p. 10, 1.2.9; p. 51, "Loading the Trailer"; p. 54, 4.3.1; p. 55, 4.3.1.1-4.3.1.2
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
8. Strap the load to the tie-down loops, raise and latch the ramp gate, and put a linchpin in the latch if it has a hole for one. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual, p. 11, 1.2.11; p. 55, 4.3.1.2
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
9. Every 50 miles or hour of towing, stop and check the coupler, chains, load and latches.
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual, p. 58, "Make Regular Stops"
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
10. To unhitch: block the tires, unplug, unhook the chains, open the coupler, make sure the ground under the jack will hold, then wind the jack down to lift the tongue. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual, p. 41, "Uncoupling the Ball Hitch Trailer with Tongue Jack"
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
