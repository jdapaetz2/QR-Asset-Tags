## Quick guide (renter)

1. Check that the tow vehicle's hitch ball is 2-5/16 inches and rated for at least 14,000 lb, and that the ball, hitch and coupler have no cracks, wear or looseness. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), p. 6, 1.2.4 warning; pp. 41-43, "Trailer with Ball-Hitch Coupler"; 14LP Heavy Duty Ultra Low Profile Dump (product page), Features & Options, Coupler
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**: Sourced; knowledgeable review outstanding.
2. Lower the coupler fully onto the ball, close the latch and put the pin or lock through it. Test it: raise the rear of the tow vehicle 1 inch with the trailer jack (no more), then lower and raise the jack fully. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), p. 43, "Prepare the coupler and hitch"; p. 44, "Couple the trailer to the tow vehicle"
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
3. Cross the safety chains under the coupler and hook them to the tow vehicle's frame with the S-hooks put in from underneath; slack to turn, not to drag. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), p. 7, 1.2.5; pp. 44-45, "Rig the safety chains"
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
4. Clip the breakaway cable to the tow vehicle (not to a chain or the hitch) so it pulls before the chains go tight. Test it: pull the pin and try to pull the trailer forward; you should feel the brakes. Put the pin straight back. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), p. 8, 1.2.6; pp. 45-46, "Attach and test electric breakaway brake system"
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
5. Connect the 7-way plug and check the tail, brake and turn lights. Then, moving at under 5 mph, apply the brake controller by hand and feel the trailer brakes work. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), p. 12, 1.2.13; pp. 46-47, "Connect the Electrical Cables"
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
6. Look for tire damage, set the pressure printed on the VIN label (front left) while the tires are cold, and check that the lug nuts are tight. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), pp. 8-9, 1.2.8
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**: Torque value unavailable (see spec lug-torque).
7. Load with the trailer hitched and on level ground. Stay within the payload on the trailer's decal (listed cargo capacity 9,820 lb). Spread loose material evenly front to back and side to side; secure anything that is not loose material. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), p. 62, 4.4.1-4.4.2; p. 63, 4.4.3-4.4.5; 14LP Heavy Duty Ultra Low Profile Dump (product page), Models & Dimensions, 14LP-14
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
8. Before dumping: trailer level and on firm ground, area clear of people, no power lines overhead. Open the rear doors and chain them to the sides, then open the control box. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), p. 63, danger box; p. 64, 4.4.6; p. 67, power-line warning
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
9. Standing well clear, hold UP until the body is about halfway up, then stop and check behind the trailer for room. Raise to about three-quarters and check again. Never leave the controls while the body is up. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), p. 64, 4.4.6
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
10. If the load sticks, do not jerk the button or brake hard to shake it loose. Lower the body and free the load by hand. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), p. 63, danger box; p. 64, 4.4.6; p. 67
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
11. When empty: lower the body fully, close and latch the doors, and lock the hoist controls before you move. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), p. 63, danger box; p. 64, 4.4.6; p. 67
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
12. Every 50 miles or hour of towing, stop and check the coupler, chains, load and doors.
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), p. 68, "Make Regular Stops"
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
13. To unhitch: block the tires, unplug, unclip the breakaway cable, unhook the chains, open the coupler, check the ground under the jack, then lower the jack to take the tongue weight. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), p. 47, "Uncoupling the Ball Hitch Trailer with Tongue Jack"
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**

## Warnings (renter)

1. Never go under a raised dump body. The body prop is for maintenance and holds an empty body only. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), pp. 64-65, 4.4.8 "Body Prop"; p. 67
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
2. If the hoist will not lift the load, unload some by hand. Never help the hoist with a jack, crane or other machine. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), p. 63, danger box
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
3. Do not fully raise a loaded body: the tow vehicle's rear wheels can lose traction. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), p. 67, warning
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
4. A raised body near power lines can electrocute without touching them. Check overhead before raising. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), p. 67, warning
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
5. Do not move the trailer until the coupler is locked, the chains and breakaway cable are attached and the jack is fully raised. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), p. 7, warning under 1.2.4
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
6. Never carry people, or flammable, explosive or poisonous materials. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), p. 12, 1.2.12; p. 63
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**

## Support notes (staff only, never public)

1. "The box won't go up" with the pump only clicking or silent: the hoist manual's first check is that the battery is fully charged.
   - Source: Hoist Manual, p. 24, Trouble Shooting Guide, problem 1
   - Status: **BLOCKED**: The Hoist Manual does not list the 14LP; Big Tex has not confirmed it applies.
2. Loaded body will not rise all the way: the hoist manual says to check all electrical connections (ground and positive) are tight.
   - Source: Hoist Manual, p. 24, Trouble Shooting Guide, problem 2
   - Status: **BLOCKED**: Same applicability question.
3. Empty body will not rise all the way: the hoist manual says to check the reservoir fluid with the bed down (1 inch from the top). Hydraulic work goes to a qualified technician.
   - Source: Hoist Manual, p. 24, Trouble Shooting Guide, problem 3; Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), p. 64, 4.4.7
   - Status: **BLOCKED**: Same applicability question.
4. Hoist will not lift the load: reduce the load by hand and get service from a qualified hydraulics technician. Never assist the hoist. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), p. 63, danger box
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
5. Lights or brakes dead: check the 7-way connection (an improper connection leaves the lights and electric brakes inoperable). Staff can compare the contacts with Big Tex's 7-way RV sheet; the two Big Tex sheets label the black and blue wires differently, so confirm on the trailer.
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), p. 47, warning on electrical connection; Standard Big Tex Electrical Connector Wiring Diagrams (Plug Contacts), 7-Way RV; Standard Big Tex Electrical Connector Wiring Diagrams (Wiring Diagram), 7 Way
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
6. Breakaway test fails: do not tow until the brakes or the breakaway battery are repaired. The battery runs down fast while the pin is out. *(safety-critical)*
   - Source: Big Tex Trailers Owners Manual (Dump Trailers), p. 46
   - Status: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**

### Quick start · REVIEW_REQUIRED

> 1. Ball: 2-5/16 inch, rated 14,000 lb or more. Seat the coupler, latch it and pin it.
> 2. Lift-test: raise the truck 1 inch with the jack (no more), then raise the jack fully.
> 3. Cross the safety chains under the coupler and hook them to the truck's frame.
> 4. Clip the breakaway cable to the truck, not to a chain. Test: pull the pin, feel the brakes hold, put the pin straight back.
> 5. Plug in the 7-way and check the lights. Then, under 5 mph, apply the brake controller and feel the trailer brakes.
> 6. Tires: no damage, pressure as printed on the VIN label (front left), lug nuts tight.
> 7. Load hitched and level, within the payload decal (9,820 lb listed). Spread loose material evenly.
> 8. To dump: level, firm ground, people clear, no power lines overhead. Chain the doors open, raise in stages and check behind.
> The Start-Up Guide shows each step.
