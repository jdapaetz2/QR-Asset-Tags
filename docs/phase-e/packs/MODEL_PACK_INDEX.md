## Tiers

- **premium-showcase**: Everything in a standard pack, plus original pictographic panels and a Start-Up Guide PDF, retouched licensed photos, staff support notes and a showcase story. Planned for all four Northridge showcase assets (E2A); a pack whose panels are all blocked is listed as standard until they can be drawn.
- **standard-onboarding**: Identity and configuration, sourced specs, the official manual link (or hosting where permitted), page text condensed from cited steps, the customer's own photo and the matching return template. No new artwork: a premium pack's panels are reused only for the same model and configuration, and only after the operator approves cross-organization reuse.
